# TalentBridge “What’s new” promo: script and sound brief

For whoever adds the sound (person or AI agent). The video is finished and silent; this file says what to add and exactly when. **Don’t change the picture.** The audio has to fit the video as it is.

- **Video:** `talentbridge-whats-new.mp4`, 1920×1080, 30 fps, **54.8 s**, H.264, no audio track.
- **Machine-readable timings:** `sound-cues.json`, with every scene’s start and end, its on-screen text, and each sound-effect moment in seconds. It’s generated from the video itself, so it’s exact.
- **Where it’s posted:** X (Twitter). Most people watch muted at first, so the on-screen text already tells the whole story. Sound should add energy and warmth, not carry information the picture lacks.

## Brand voice
Warm, confident, plain-spoken. It sounds like someone on the job seeker’s side, not hype. Short sentences. TalentBridge’s tagline is “AI that advocates for people.”

## Music direction
- **Feel:** upbeat, warm and hopeful; modern and clean, not corporate or cheesy. No vocals, or vocals far under the VO.
- **Tempo:** the scene changes fall on bar lines at **100 BPM in 4/4** (one bar = 2.4 s). A 100 BPM track will hit every cut on a downbeat.
- **Shape:**
  - 0.0–4.8: quiet and sparse; build tension under the hook.
  - At 4.8: the beat comes in as the logo appears.
  - 24.0–27.0: drop the drums or thin out, and build.
  - At 27.0: an impact on the level-up, then full energy.
  - 46.8 on: the final call to action; resolve on a final hit or chord by about 52.8, with a tail that’s silent by 54.8.
- **Licensing:** it must be cleared for commercial use on X. Keep the license or its source with the files.

## Mix and delivery
- **With voiceover:** duck the music 8–10 dB under the voice. The VO must be clearly intelligible on phone speakers.
- **Loudness:** −14 LUFS integrated, true peak no higher than −1 dBTP. Stereo, 48 kHz.
- **Length:** exactly 54.8 s. Don’t trim or extend the video.
- **Final file:** add the sound to the silent video without re-encoding the picture:

```bash
ffmpeg -i talentbridge-whats-new.mp4 -i audio.wav \
  -map 0:v -map 1:a -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 192k -ar 48000 \
  -shortest -movflags +faststart talentbridge-whats-new-with-sound.mp4
```

- **Check before handing back:** play it through. The level-up at 27.0 should land on the chime and impact, and “rise” in the VO. Every line should finish before its scene ends.

## Re-rendering the video
The picture is drawn from `video.html`. To change on-screen text or timing, edit it and run `node brand/video/render.cjs full`. That rebuilds the MP4 and `sound-cues.json`. If timings change, update the tables above to match.
