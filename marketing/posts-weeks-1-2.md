### Week 2, Monday: you can’t buzzword a 5
**Image:** `brand/evidentalent/capability-graph-explainer.png`

> Can AI rate skills fairly? Here’s how we make it hard to game and easy to check.
>
> On EvidenTalent, a skill’s rating depends on the evidence behind it:
>
> • Only implied by a job title → 2 at most
> • Described as a responsibility → 3 at most
> • Shown with a real example of what you did and what changed → up to 5
>
> You can’t buzzword your way to a 5. And because the scale is published, you can see exactly why you got your rating, and so can employers.
>
> We also run fairness checks on the AI: the same experience should get the same rating, no matter whose name is on it.
>
> Recruiters and hiring managers: would this help you tell candidates apart? I’d really value your take. Link in the comments.
